BASE  (9 steps, applied in this order)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. unicode_nfc
      NFC gives a character a single stored form. Ge'ez characters already have
      only one, so the step changes only Latin text, such as an accented letter
      typed two ways.

  2. strip_zero_width
      Zero-width characters are invisible and carry no text. Left in, they would
      make two equal words compare unequal.

  3. strip_bracket_tags
      No reference in these sets carries a bracket tag. The step removes the
      tags [unclear:SS.S], [overlap:SS.S], [laughter], [cough] and [noise]
      wherever they occur. None of them is scored as a word.

  4. mark_fragments
      A trailing hyphen marks a word the speaker began and did not finish, as in
      ከስን-. Punctuation removal would delete the hyphen, so this step marks the
      word first. Fluent mode then drops it from the reference. Verbatim mode
      keeps it without the hyphen.

  5. expand_abbreviations
      Readers say abbreviations in full. A reader of 2015 ዓ.ም says ዓመተ ምህረት, and
      of እ.ኤ.አ says እንደ ኤውሮፓውያን አቆጣጠር. References record what was said, and
      systems write either form. Both sides are therefore rewritten to the
      spoken form. The table lists each written form of an abbreviation, whether
      dotted, slashed, dotless or spaced. It spells each expansion as edited
      Ethiopian news text does. Ambiguous abbreviations such as ጠ/ሚ, ሚ/ር and አ.አ
      are not expanded. Each stays one word, so አ.አ is not read as two fillers.
      Where a speaker read out the letters, the reference keeps the abbreviation
      and both sides expand it alike.

  6. numerals
      References write every number in words. A system that writes the number in
      other words is wrong. A system may write it in digits instead. The digits
      are correct when they match the number's digit form. Some numbers have
      their digit form recorded with the reference, checked against the audio.
      Where ሁለት ሰዓት ተኩል is recorded as 2:30, 2:30 is correct and ሁለት ከሰላሳ counts
      as wrong words. For the other numbers, the parser reads the digit form
      from the words. It reads ዘጠኝ ሰዓት ተኩል only as 9, so 9:30 misses where no
      form was recorded. Any other digit token counts as a wrong word, Ge'ez
      numerals such as ፲፭ included. The --numerals option selects another rule,
      and the report records the choice.

  7. remove_punctuation
      Punctuation is not heard, and systems differ in whether they write it.
      Each mark becomes a word boundary. Inside a number the decimal point stays
      and a digit-group comma goes, so 10,693 stays one token.

  8. casefold
      Ge'ez has no case, so this applies to Latin script only. A capitalized
      English word inside Amharic is not an error.

  9. collapse_whitespace
      Words are split on whitespace. Repeated or trailing spaces would otherwise
      produce empty words.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLUENT  (2 steps, applied in this order)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. remove_fillers
      Fluent mode removes every filler in the inventory from both sides. A
      system that leaves fillers out is not counted wrong.

  2. remove_fragments
      Fluent mode removes unfinished words from the reference only. A system's
      words are scored as written.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERBATIM  (2 steps, applied in this order)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. unmark_fragments
      Verbatim mode keeps an unfinished word without its hyphen. A system that
      transcribes the partial word matches it.

  2. filler_equivalence
      In verbatim mode a filler matches another filler of the same language. እም
      for እ is correct. English um for Amharic እ is wrong.

WER-N EQUIVALENCE LAYER  (8 sets, 46 folds)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  h-series
      members  ሀ ሐ ኀ
      UNFOLDED ሓ  (declared and deliberately not folded)
      ሀ, ሐ and ኀ are all [h] in modern Amharic. A word's spelling follows its
      history. ሓ stays unfolded because it is not an Amharic spelling of ሃ, ሀ,
      ሐ, ኻ or ኃ.

  kha-series
      members  ሁ ኹ
      orders   ሁሂሃሄህሆ  ኹኺኻኼኽኾ
      UNFOLDED ኻ  (declared and deliberately not folded)
      The ኸ series folds to the ሀ series in orders 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7. ኸ itself
      stays distinct. ኻ stays distinct too. It is rare in written Amharic and
      mostly writes /x/ in names such as ኻርቱም and ኻሜኒ, or in Tigrinya words. It
      does not occur in the frozen references.

  ha-a-order
      members  ሀ ሃ
      orders   ሀ  ሃ
      ሃ and ሀ are written interchangeably for /ha/. Together with the h-series,
      this folds ኃ to ሀ. ሓ and ኻ stay unfolded.

  labialized-go
      members  ጎ ጐ
      orders   ጎ  ጐ
      ጐ is written for ጎ, as in ጐረቤት and ጎረቤት. ጓ is not folded.

  labialized-ko-qo-ho
      members  ቆ ቈ
      orders   ቆኮሆ  ቈኰኈ
      EthioBench extends the ጐ rule. ቈ, ኰ and ኈ are written for ቆ, ኮ and ሆ in
      the same way.

  s-series
      members  ሰ ሠ
      ሰ and ሠ are both [s]. Some words keep ሠ from their history, as in ሥራ
      "work".

  ts-series
      members  ጸ ፀ
      ጸ and ፀ are one sound. Which letter a word takes varies by region and
      schooling.

  glottal-series
      members  አ ዐ
      orders   አኡኢአኤእኦ  ዐዑዒዓዔዕዖ
      አ and ዐ are both [ʔ]. ዐ appears mainly in religious and literary writing.
      The orders are listed so that ዓ folds straight to አ. ኣ is not an Amharic
      spelling and stays unfolded. ኧ is not folded.

LOANWORD LAYER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   step     normalize_loanwords
   table    amh_loanwords.tsv
   profiles ['lenient', 'strict']
      A loanword has no settled spelling in fidel. Spellings of one word are
      folded together on both sides, whole words only. The lenient profile
      accepts every spelling the table's pattern generates. The strict profile
      accepts the canonical spelling and the spellings established in edited
      Ethiopian news text. Both are reported.
